# Products

A product is a workload built on the platform, with its own repository, its
own operational database and a tenant entry. Two example products show how
the capabilities combine; together they reach most of the platform, and
every implementation runs both as its conformance workload.

| Product | What it exercises |
| --- | --- |
| [Document review](document-review.md) | Scanned documents in, machine verdicts, human review of uncertain results, a release gate against an external register |
| [Annual census](census.md) | Registries, periodic collection from every institution, a completeness gate, indicators as at a reference date, open publication |

A new product records how it meets its capabilities in the
[product checklist](checklist.md). Like any resource, a product can be
built once the registries and products it references exist.
