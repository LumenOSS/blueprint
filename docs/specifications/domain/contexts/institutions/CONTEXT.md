# Institutions

This context covers the institutions that provide education, the sites
they work from, and the state of those sites.

## Language

**Institution**:
An organisation registered to provide education, such as a school or college, identified independently of where it operates.
_Avoid_: School (as the generic term), centre, establishment

**Site**:
A physical location where education takes place, with its buildings and services.
_Avoid_: Campus, premises, address

**Occupancy**:
The effective-dated link between an institution and a site it operates from; an institution may occupy several sites and a site may host several institutions.
_Avoid_: Location assignment

**Facility status**:
The recorded condition and availability of a service at a site, such as water, power or connectivity, as at a date.
_Avoid_: Infrastructure flag
