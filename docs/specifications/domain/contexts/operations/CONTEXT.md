# Operations

This context covers the supporting work of running institutions: money,
assets, entitlement schemes and safety. Existing generic systems often
provide it, so it's usually integrated rather than built.

## Language

**Budget**:
An approved allocation of money to an institution or function for a financial period.
_Avoid_: Allocation (unless it is a distribution)

**Asset**:
A durable item an institution holds and accounts for.
_Avoid_: Equipment, inventory item

**Entitlement scheme**:
A scheme that provides learners with a benefit under eligibility rules, such as meals, transport or a scholarship.
_Avoid_: Programme (which means a course of education here), project

**Beneficiary**:
A learner receiving an entitlement scheme's benefit during a period.
_Avoid_: Recipient, participant

**Incident**:
A recorded event affecting the safety of learners or staff at a site.
_Avoid_: Case, report
