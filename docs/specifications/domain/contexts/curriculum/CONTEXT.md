# Curriculum

What is taught: programmes, grades, subjects and the classes they are taught in.

## Language

**Programme**:
A coherent course of education at an institution, mapped to a level of the international classification of education.
_Avoid_: Course, track, stream

**Grade**:
A stage within a programme that a learner normally completes in one academic year.
_Avoid_: Year group, standard, class

**Subject**:
A field of study taught as a unit within a programme.
_Avoid_: Course (unless it is a course offering)

**Class**:
A group of learners taught together in an academic period, which may span grades.
_Avoid_: Section, homeroom
