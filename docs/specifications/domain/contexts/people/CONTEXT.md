# People

Who a person is, recorded once, whatever roles they hold.

## Language

**Person**:
A human being recorded once in the person registry, whatever roles they hold.
_Avoid_: Individual, user, record

**Organisation**:
A body that is not a person, such as an agency, that can hold a role such as guardian.
_Avoid_: Entity, party

**External identifier**:
An identifier issued by another authority and held as an attribute of a person or institution.
_Avoid_: National ID as a key, reference number
