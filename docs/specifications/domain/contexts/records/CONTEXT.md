# Records

How long records are kept, how they are corrected, and how a person's own records reach them.

## Language

**Record class**:
A group of records sharing a retention period, trigger event and legal basis.
_Avoid_: Retention category

**Disposal**:
The scheduled, audited deletion of a record class at the end of its retention.
_Avoid_: Purge, cleanup

**Correction request**:
A request to change a recorded value, with its reason, evidence and approval; it produces a new version.
_Avoid_: Edit, fix, update

**Individual disclosure**:
The release of a person's own records to that person, their guardian or an authorised agency.
_Avoid_: Export, data request, extract

**Purpose**:
The declared reason a personal-data element is collected or a record is accessed.
_Avoid_: Use case, justification
