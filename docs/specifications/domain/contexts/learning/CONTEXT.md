# Learning

This context covers a learner's part in education: their enrolment,
class membership, attendance and progression, and the guardians who act
for them. It's the core of an EMIS.

## Language

**Learner**:
A person in the role of someone enrolled, or once enrolled, in education.
_Avoid_: Student, pupil (except inside an official indicator name)

**Guardian**:
A person or a body with legal authority for a learner.
_Avoid_: Parent (unless the authority is parental), caregiver, carer

**Guardian relationship**:
The effective-dated link between a learner and their guardian, which ends when the legal authority ends.
_Avoid_: Contact, next of kin

**Enrolment**:
The effective-dated record of a learner registered at an institution in a programme and grade for an academic period.
_Avoid_: Registration, admission

**Main enrolment**:
The one enrolment that counts a learner for an institution at a given moment; any other concurrent enrolment is secondary.
_Avoid_: Primary enrolment, home school

**Transfer**:
The end of a learner's enrolment at one institution with exit reason 'transferred', followed by an enrolment at another.
_Avoid_: Move, migration

**Class membership**:
The effective-dated record of a learner belonging to a class.
_Avoid_: Class enrolment

**Exit reason**:
Why an enrolment ended: completed, transferred, left the education system, or deceased; distinct from the enrolment's state.
_Avoid_: Dropout status

**Progression decision**:
The recorded outcome for a learner at the end of an academic period: promote, repeat, complete or leave.
_Avoid_: Promotion status, result

**Attendance session**:
One recorded occasion on which a learner was expected to be present, with whether they were.
_Avoid_: Roll call, register mark
