# Assessment

This context covers tests and examinations: who sat them, and the
results they got.

## Language

**Assessment instrument**:
A versioned test or examination, with its items, scoring rules and proficiency thresholds.
_Avoid_: Exam paper, test form

**Administration**:
One sitting of an assessment instrument by a group of candidates on a date.
_Avoid_: Sitting, session (unless that is the local term)

**Candidate**:
A learner entered for an administration.
_Avoid_: Examinee, test taker

**Result**:
A candidate's recorded outcome for an administration; once released, a change is a new version.
_Avoid_: Score (unless it is a raw score), mark

**Accommodation**:
An approved adjustment to how a candidate sits an administration.
_Avoid_: Special arrangement, concession
